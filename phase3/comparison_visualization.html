📊 Key Findings & Recommendations

  • Best for Payee Names: Qwen 7b (60% exact, 0.084 CER) — excels at reading names
  • Best for Amount Figures: TrOCR Improved (20% exact, 0.20 CER) — when it works, it's accurate
  • Best for Amount-in-words: Qwen 7b (20% exact, 0.307 CER) — much better than TrOCR/Qwen 3b
  • Trade-off: TrOCR uses fine-tuning but overfits; Qwen uses pre-trained models with better generalization
  • Recommendation: Use Qwen 7b for production (better on payee, faster on amount-words) or ensemble both models for different fields
